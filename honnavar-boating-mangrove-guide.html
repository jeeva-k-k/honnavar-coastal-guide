Estuary Ecology & Backwater Cruises

Navigating the Sharavathi River & Mangrove Sanctuaries

Honnavar is famous across Karnataka for the pristine meeting of the Sharavathi River with the Arabian Sea. The resulting tidal backwaters host dense mangrove forests, scenic river islands, and dramatic views of the historic railway bridge. Experiencing both the elevated Kandla Mangrove Boardwalk and a private river boat cruise is the signature itinerary for visitors.

1. Sharavathi Backwater Boating Point & Token Counters

  • Departure Location: Kasarkod River Jetty (just 2.1 km from Lopez Paradise Homestay).
  • Tokenized Boarding System: Tokens are issued at authorized token points. Visitors collect their token first, then proceed directly to the boat boarding point.
  • Cruise Duration: Choose between the 1-hour package (₹1,500) or the 2-hour package (₹2,500).
  • Best Hours: Late afternoon (4:00 PM – 5:30 PM) for sunset views, or morning (8:00 AM – 9:30 AM) for tranquil waters.

2. Kandla Mangrove Nature Boardwalk

  • Distance from Basecamp: Exactly 3.4 km by car (or 2.6 km via scenic bike trail).
  • Trail Experience: An elevated wooden suspended walkway traversing through Rhizophora and Avicennia mangrove swamps. Entry fee is ₹20 per person.
  • Visitor Timings: 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM daily.

Frequently Asked Questions: Honnavar Boating & Mangroves

How much does Honnavar boating cost and how does the token system work?

Honnavar boating is governed by an official tokenized system with no individual per-person charges. It has two fixed tiers: ₹1,500 for a 1-hour safari and ₹2,500 for a 2-hour safari. Travelers purchase a token at the token collection counters located nearby before heading to the boarding spot.

What are the operating timings for boating in Honnavar?

Boats operate daily from 7:00 AM to 6:30 PM. Sunset rides between 4:30 PM and 6:00 PM are the most sought-after for calm estuarine breezes and golden sunset skies over the Arabian Sea.

Which homestay is closest to Kasarkod Eco Beach and Kandla Mangrove Boardwalk?

Lopez Paradise Homestay is located in Pragati Nagar, Kasarkod (PIN 581342), just 2.1 km from the Kasarkod boating jetty, 3.4 km from Kasarkod Blue Flag Eco Beach, and 3.4 km driving distance from the Kandla Mangrove Boardwalk.
