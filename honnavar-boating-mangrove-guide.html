Estuary Ecology & Backwater Cruises

Navigating the Sharavathi River & Mangrove Sanctuaries

Honnavar is famous across Karnataka for the pristine meeting of the Sharavathi River with the Arabian Sea. The resulting tidal backwaters host dense mangrove forests, scenic river islands, and dramatic views of the historic railway bridge. Experiencing both the elevated Kandla Mangrove Boardwalk and a private river boat cruise is the signature itinerary for visitors.

1. Sharavathi Backwater Boating Point

  • Departure Location: Kasarkod River Jetty (just 2.1 km from Lopez Paradise Homestay).
  • Cruise Duration: 45 to 60 minutes covering the mangrove channels and river estuary.
  • Best Hours: Late afternoon (4:00 PM – 5:30 PM) for sunset views, or morning (8:00 AM – 9:30 AM) for tranquil waters.

2. Kandla Mangrove Nature Boardwalk

  • Distance from Basecamp: Exactly 3.4 km by car (or 2.6 km via scenic bike trail).
  • Trail Experience: An elevated wooden suspended walkway traversing through Rhizophora and Avicennia mangrove swamps.
  • Visitor Timings: 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM daily.

Why Base at Lopez Paradise Homestay for Water & Mangrove Tours?

The majority of river tour departure points and the mangrove boardwalk are concentrated on the southern bank in Kasarkod. Staying across the river in Honnavar town requires navigating the busy NH 66 bridge each time. By staying at Lopez Paradise Homestay in Pragati Nagar, Kasarkod:

  • You are within a 5-minute drive of both the boating jetty and the mangrove walk.
  • You return to private, clean air-conditioned rooms with hot water showers after walking the wetlands.
  • Your vehicle is safely parked in the private, gated on-site parking area.
