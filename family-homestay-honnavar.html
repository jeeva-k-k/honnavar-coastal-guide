Why Families Choose Kasarkod Over Town Center Stays

When traveling along Coastal Karnataka with children, elderly parents, or large family groups, downtown town-center hotels frequently suffer from heavy NH 66 traffic noise, congested curb parking, and cramped commercial room layouts. Lopez Paradise Homestay in Pragati Nagar, Kasarkod provides a tranquil, gated residential campus surrounded by greenery, situated just 3.4 km from the Blue Flag Kasarkod Eco Beach and 2.1 km from the Sharavathi backwater boating jetty.

Frequently Asked Questions for Family Travelers

Is advance booking required for whole-property family groups?

Yes. For families requiring all 7 rooms to host 12 to 18 guests in complete privacy, advance confirmation is strongly recommended, especially over long weekends, Diwali, and the December winter holidays.

What is the check-in and check-out schedule?

Standard check-in is after 11:00 AM and check-out is by 10:00 AM. Early check-in or late luggage drop can be arranged with host Mani based on room availability.

Can private tour buses and tempo travelers turn around inside?

Yes. Pragati Nagar has wide access roads leading to the gated driveway of Lopez Paradise, allowing tempo travelers and 14-seater minibuses to safely enter, turn around, and park securely inside the property boundary.
