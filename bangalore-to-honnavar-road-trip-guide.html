Stage 1: Bangalore to Tumakuru (NH 48) • ~70 km

Early morning departure (5:30–6:00 AM) to beat city traffic along the Tumkur expressway. Smooth 6-lane tollway with numerous modern fuel and breakfast stops.

Stage 2: Tumakuru to Shivamogga via Arsikere & Tarikere • ~210 km

Well-maintained 4-lane and 2-lane state highways through coconut groves and rural towns. Excellent spot for a traditional South Indian filter coffee and masala dosa breakfast.

Stage 3: Shivamogga to Sagara & Jog Falls (NH 69) • ~100 km

Ascend into the lush green Sharavathi valley. Make a 45-minute stop at iconic Jog Falls (Raja, Roarer, Rocket, and Rani cascades) located right along the highway.

Stage 4: Jog Falls to Honnavar / Kasarkod • ~60 km

A breathtaking descent through the Gersoppa Ghats, winding through dense teak and evergreen canopies along the Sharavathi River, arriving at Kasarkod, Honnavar.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are roads suitable for small hatchbacks and sedans?

Yes. The entire route along NH 48 and NH 69 is fully asphalted and suitable for all vehicle types, including sedans and low-ground-clearance cars.

What time should we leave Bangalore?

Departing by 5:30 AM allows you to pass Tumakuru before rush hour, reach Shivamogga for a leisurely lunch, explore Jog Falls by 2:30 PM, and arrive at Lopez Paradise in Kasarkod by 5:00 PM in time for sunset at Kasarkod Eco Beach.
