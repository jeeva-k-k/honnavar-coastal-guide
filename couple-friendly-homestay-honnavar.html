Frequently Asked Questions: Honnavar Homestay for Couples

Is Lopez Paradise Homestay suitable for couples?

Yes. Lopez Paradise offers 5 private Deluxe Couple Rooms equipped with split air conditioning, king beds, ensuite modern washrooms, 24/7 hot water, high-speed Wi-Fi, and a quiet garden setting in Kasarkod.

How far is the beach for sunset walks?

Kasarkod Blue Flag Eco Beach is just 3.4 km away (6 minutes by car or scooter), offering uncrowded, pristine white sands and stunning Arabian Sea sunsets.

What activities can couples do nearby?

Couples can enjoy sunset boat rides along the Sharavathi backwaters (2.1 km departure jetty), nature walks along the Kandla Mangrove Boardwalk (3.4 km), and visits to the nearby Apsarakonda cliff-top viewpoint.
