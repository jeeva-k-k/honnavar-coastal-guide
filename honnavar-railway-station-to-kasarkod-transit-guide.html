Step-by-Step Route: Honnavar Railway Station to Lopez Paradise Kasarkod

1

Exit Station & Auto Stand (0.0 km)

Upon exiting Platform 1 at Honnavar Railway Station (HNVR), you will find the designated local auto-rickshaw queue. Standard fares to Kasarkod are pre-fixed by local auto driver associations (₹160 to ₹200 daytime, ₹250 to ₹300 past 9:00 PM).

2

Join National Highway 66 (1.5 km)

Head down Station Road to intersect NH 66 (Panvel - Kochi Highway). Turn left heading south toward Kasarkod, Murudeshwar, and Bhatkal.

3

Cross the Historic Sharavathi River Bridge (3.5 km)

Cross the iconic 2-km Sharavathi bridge, enjoying panoramic views of the river estuary, mangrove islands, and the railway bridge to the east. Honnavar port and the Kasarkod boat jetty lie immediately on the south bank.

4

Turn off NH 66 into Pragati Nagar, Kelginuru (6.8 km)

Pass the Kasarkod Eco Beach arch junction and turn right into Pragati Nagar, Kelginuru. Lopez Paradise Homestay is located 400 meters inside, featuring a secure private gated compound with parking for 6+ cars and SUVs (PIN: 581342).

Frequently Asked Transit & Station Questions

Are auto-rickshaws available at Honnavar railway station for late-night arrivals?

Yes, but availability can be limited between midnight and 5:00 AM. If you are arriving on late-night trains like the Netravati Express (approx. 01:50 AM), we strongly advise messaging host Mani at Lopez Paradise (+91 8792466846) prior to boarding. The host will have a verified local auto driver waiting for you at the station exit.

How do I take a public bus from Honnavar station to Kasarkod?

Take an auto from the railway station to Honnavar Town KSRTC Bus Stand (3 km, approx. ₹60-₹80). From the bus stand, board any local bus heading toward Bhatkal or Murudeshwar and ask for the Kasarkod Eco Beach Cross stop (ticket is approx. ₹15). From the highway cross, Lopez Paradise is an easy 5-minute walk.

What is the pin code and exact location of Lopez Paradise Homestay?

The official address is Pragati Nagar, Kelginuru, Kasarkod, Honnavar, Karnataka - 581342 (coordinates: 14.244209° N, 74.446930° E). Do not use the outdated 581334 town pin code, which directs navigation toward the inland town center instead of the beach estuary.

Can guests park large SUVs securely at Lopez Paradise?

Yes. Unlike many shacks and cramped stays in Gokarna or Honnavar town, Lopez Paradise features a private, gated, on-site compound with dedicated secure parking for 6+ cars and large SUVs (such as Fortuner, Scorpio-N, XUV700, Harrier).
