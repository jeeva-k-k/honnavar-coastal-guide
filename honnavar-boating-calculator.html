Understanding the Government Token Boating System

Over the past month, Honnavar backwater boating has officially transitioned to a tokenized government-regulated system to eliminate unregulated touts and inconsistent pricing:

  • Strictly No Per-Person Charges: Boat rides are flat rates per boat for up to the designated passenger capacity (~8 to 10 people).
  • Fixed Tiers: ₹1,500 for a 1-hour cruise or ₹2,500 for a 2-hour extended cruise through the mangrove tunnels and river mouth.
  • Token Collection: Visitors must collect their token at designated token counters before walking to the boarding point.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Lopez Paradise help arrange the boating tokens?

Yes. Host Mani coordinates directly with the local boat operators and directs guests to the nearest official token counter (just 2.1 km away), ensuring convenient departure slots.

Are life jackets provided on the boats?

Life jackets are not provided by all boat operators in Honnavar; only a select few boats currently carry them on board. If you are traveling with non-swimmers, seniors, or small children, we strongly advise requesting a boat equipped with life jackets at the token counter or confirming safety gear prior to boarding at the jetty.
