Kasarkod Eco Beach

A broad Arabian Sea beach for coastal walks, open horizons and sunset visits.

02 · 3.8 km

Kasarkod Beach

An open shoreline near the Sharavathi river mouth, suited to unhurried beach time.

03 · 2.1 km

Backwater Boating

Calm estuary channels, palm-lined river scenery and local boating experiences.

04 · 2.6 km

Kandla Mangroves

A waterside boardwalk through mangrove habitat with estuary views and birdlife.

05 · 1.7 km

Apsarakonda

A coastal outing combining beach, freshwater falls and elevated viewpoints.

06 · 10.6 km

Idagunji Temple

A renowned Ganesha temple in the heritage village landscape south-east of Honnavar.

07 · 4.1 km · 12 min by car

Mugali Beach

One of Honnavar's beautiful hidden beaches, with a rocky shoreline and a quiet Arabian Sea setting. The primary route is via Kelagin Kasarkod.

Honnavar KSRTC bus stand is approximately 6.2 km away, with onward local transport toward Kasarkod.
