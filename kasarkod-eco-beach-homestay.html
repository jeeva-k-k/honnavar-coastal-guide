Experience Karnataka's Cleanest Certified Eco Beach

Kasarkod Eco Beach is globally certified under the international Blue Flag program, awarded for its strict environmental criteria, pristine white sand dunes, solar-powered lighting, clean changing rooms, and disciplined safety standards. Located on the southern bank of the Sharavathi River estuary, it stands in stark contrast to crowded commercial shores.

Verified Distance & Transit Route

From Lopez Paradise Homestay in Pragati Nagar, Kasarkod Blue Flag Beach is exactly 3.4 km away:

  • By Car / Bike: 6 to 8 minutes via the scenic Kasarkod coastal road.
  • On Foot / Bicycle: 35-minute walking trail through serene village coconut groves.
  • Beach Timings: 6:00 AM to 6:30 PM daily (Lifeguards stationed during daylight hours).
