The Geography of the Sharavathi River

The Sharavathi River creates one of India's most extraordinary natural geographic spectacles. It originates in the Western Ghats, plunges 253 meters (830 feet) down the cliff face at Jog Falls, snakes through the lush Gersoppa Valley, and flows into the Arabian Sea at Honnavar.

By pairing Jog Falls with Honnavar, you experience the complete life of the river: from dramatic waterfall cascades to tranquil estuarine mangroves and peaceful coastal sunsets.

Curated 2-Day Circuit Itinerary

Day 1 (Morning): Jog Falls Viewpoints

Witness Raja, Roarer, Rocket, and Rani falls from the main viewing decks and British Bungalow. Morning lighting between 9:00 AM and 11:30 AM offers optimal photography conditions.

Day 1 (Afternoon): Scenic Drive down to Honnavar

Take NH 69 through the lush Gersoppa Valley. Check in at Lopez Paradise Homestay in Kasarkod by 2:30 PM. Freshen up with continuous hot water and air-conditioned suites.

Day 1 (Late Afternoon / Sunset): Kasarkod Blue Flag Eco Beach

Drive 3.4 km to Kasarkod Eco Beach. Enjoy pristine white sands, clean walking promenades, and golden coastal sunsets away from commercial noise.

Day 2 (Morning): Mangrove Walk & Backwater Boating

Enjoy fresh complimentary Neer Dosa breakfast at 8:00 AM. Visit the Kandla Mangrove Boardwalk (₹20 entry fee) followed by a 1-hour or 2-hour government-tokenized Sharavathi river safari (₹1,500 / ₹2,500 per boat).

Day 2 (Afternoon): Apsarakonda Falls & Onward Journey

Visit Apsarakonda Falls and the cliff sea viewpoint (4.8 km from homestay) before continuing onward to Gokarna (48 km) or returning to Bangalore/Pune.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to drive the Jog Falls to Honnavar road at night?

The road is well-paved, but it winds through the Sharavathi Ghats with minimal street lighting. We recommend descending before sunset (by 6:30 PM) for the best views and easiest driving experience.

Can large family groups be accommodated at Lopez Paradise?

Yes. The property can host up to 18 guests across 7 air-conditioned rooms, making it ideal for family groups traveling together.
