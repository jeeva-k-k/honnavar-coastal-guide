Frequently Asked Questions: Vegetarian Travel in Honnavar

Is pure vegetarian food available at homestays in Honnavar?
Yes. At Lopez Paradise Homestay, guests enjoy a complimentary daily South Indian vegetarian breakfast served fresh between 8:00 AM and 9:30 AM, featuring hot neer dosa, idli sambar, upma, and authentic filter coffee prepared with spotless hygiene.
Are there good pure veg restaurants near Kasarkod and Honnavar town?
Yes. Honnavar town has several respected pure vegetarian restaurants serving Udupi-style thalis and tiffins, located just 5 to 7 minutes drive across the Sharavathi bridge from Lopez Paradise Homestay.
Can guest families self-cook in the homestay?
To maintain pristine hygiene and allergy safety for all guests, guest self-cooking is not permitted. However, infant milk heating and dietary assistance for elders are warmly accommodated by the hosts.
