Sharavathi Kandla Mangrove Boardwalk Field Data (2026 Guide)

The Kandla Mangrove Boardwalk is one of Coastal Karnataka's most enchanting eco-tourism destinations. Built above the tidal waters of the Sharavathi estuary, this raised wooden pathway allows visitors to walk right through dense, untouched mangrove thickets without disturbing the delicate estuarine habitat.

🕒 Operating Timings

Open daily from 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM. Entry tickets are issued at the gate by the Forest Department. Early mornings are best for calm water reflections and cool breezes.

🦅 Avian Biodiversity

A paradise for nature enthusiasts and photographers. Look out for Brahminy Kites, White-bellied Sea Eagles, Pied Kingfishers, and mudskippers in the exposed roots during low tide.

🚤 Sharavathi Boating Connection

Pair your boardwalk walk with a private boat ride along the Sharavathi backwaters, passing beneath the railway bridges and winding through river channels.

🚗 Convenient Access

Located right off the coastal highway, with direct access via car, two-wheeler, or cycle from Kasarkod across the Sharavathi bridge.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the entry ticket price for Kandla Mangrove Boardwalk?

The entry ticket fee is strictly ₹20 per person, collected at the Karnataka Forest Department entrance gate counter.

How far is Lopez Paradise from the Kandla Mangrove Boardwalk?

Lopez Paradise is located 3.4 km by car (a scenic 6-minute drive across the Sharavathi bridge) and 2.6 km via the coastal cycling/walking trail.

Can the homestay help arrange boat rides and explain boating pricing?

Yes. Sharavathi backwater boating is officially regulated by the government under a tokenized system with no per-person tickets: ₹1,500 for a 1-hour safari and ₹2,500 for a 2-hour safari (full boat). The host can guide guests to the nearby token collection counters before they proceed to the Kasarkod boating departure point (2.1 km away).
